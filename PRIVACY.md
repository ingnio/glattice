# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 26, 2026

Glattice is a local desktop application. This policy explains what data
the app accesses, what it stores, and what it never does.

## What Glattice accesses

| Data | Why | Leaves your device? |
|------|-----|---------------------|
| Screen content | To record and display frames | No |
| Microphone audio | Optional; to add audio to exports | No |
| Local file system | To save and load recordings, export files | No |
| Clipboard | To paste exported content | No |

## What Glattice never does

- Sends recordings, screenshots, or audio to any server
- Collects usage analytics or telemetry
- Tracks keystrokes beyond displaying them on-screen as requested
- Creates accounts, syncs data, or requires a network connection
- Accesses any file outside paths you explicitly open or configure

## Network activity

The only network access is a one-time download of the Microsoft
WebView2 bootstrapper during installation on Windows systems that do not
already have WebView2. This is a Microsoft download and is subject to
[Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com). No
Glattice data is transmitted in this process.

## Local storage

Recordings and edits are stored in your system's app cache directory.
You can delete them at any time through the application or your
operating system's file manager.

## Your responsibilities

Because Glattice can capture screen content and audio, you are
responsible for complying with applicable recording consent laws in your
jurisdiction before recording other people or their content. See
Section 3 of the EULA for details.
